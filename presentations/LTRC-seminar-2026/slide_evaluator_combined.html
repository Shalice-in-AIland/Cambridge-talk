This study — design at a glance
Can AI speaking feedback quote the learner safely, when its only evidence is an imperfect ASR transcript?
DESIGN
106 real learner dialogues, human-transcribed; two prompts (simple quoting request vs governed quoting strategy); 2 ASR engines; 4 LLMs; every quoted phrase verified in code against the transcript and the human reference.
KEY FINDINGS
  • Simple request: 1 in 3 messages misquoted the learner.
  • Governed strategy: 1 in 25 — safety comes from quoting less, and only when sure.
  • The newer ASR engine did not reduce wrong quotes (14 vs 14).
  • Model choice matters: one frontier model defeated the rules.
