Unpacking IC
1 · ARCHITECTURE CEILING
Pre-LLM SDS: IC structurally capped
  • IC scored substantially lower with an SDS partner than a human partner; fluency, pronunciation, and grammar largely held steady.
  • Turn-based architecture cannot deliver backchannel, repair, or contingent timing — the architecture is the construct decision.
2 · PARTIAL UNLOCK
LLM-backed SDS: some IC opens, some stays shut
  • Now elicits topic development and sequence expansion.
  • Backchannelling remains constrained by turn-based architectures, alongside inter-turn gaps and contingent uptake.

What the corpus has not yet answered
  • Will better technology (e.g., native duplex SDS) help realise interactional competence in AI-mediated speaking?
