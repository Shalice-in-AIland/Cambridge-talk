✗  Cannot reach — what didn't land, and why The architecture beneath cannot honour what the prompt asks for.

§5.1 Don't respond too quickly — 1–2s pause  ✗

Why: the LLM doesn't experience time. The pause = VAD threshold + inference + TTS startup — set by the orchestration layer, not the prompt.

§5.2 "Still thinking" vs "finished"  ✗

Why: ① the LLM runs only after VAD silence — it isn't listening during the turn; ② the transcript has already lost the prosody that signals word-search vs turn-end. Glasson (2026): predictive competence is architecturally out of reach.

⚠  Honoured — but not by the prompt Two prompt instructions look honoured — only because the architecture cannot violate them.

§5.4 Don't interrupt mid-turn  ⚠ auto-satisfied

Why: the LLM only runs after VAD silence — it architecturally cannot cut in, whether the prompt says so or not.

§5.5 Don't finish their sentences  ⚠ auto-satisfied

Why: the LLM never sees mid-utterance speech — it cannot complete what it hasn't yet received.
