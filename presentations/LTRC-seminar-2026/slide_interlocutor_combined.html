Sim, Kim, & Ku (2025) — paper details
Exploring generative AI as a roleplay interlocutor in L2 task-based pragmatics learning. TESOL Quarterly, 59(S1), S86–S116.
DESIGN
40 Korean EFL learners; 20 paired with ChatGPT, 20 with a peer. Scenario-based roleplays (requests + refusals). ChatGPT served as interlocutor, evaluator, and mediator simultaneously.
KEY FINDINGS
  • ChatGPT group: significantly more pragmatic-related episodes (PREs).
  • Requests: short-term gains favoured ChatGPT; long-term retention favoured peer.
  • Refusals: only the ChatGPT group showed significant gains.
